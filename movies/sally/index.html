The Secret World ofArrietty.

Studio Ghibli

Release Date: 2010

Synopsis: The story follows Arrietty, a 14-year-old girl who belongs to a tiny species of people called "Borrowers". They live quietly hidden beneath the floorboards and inside the walls of a traditional suburban house, surviving by stealthily "borrowing" small everyday items from humans—like a single sugar cube or a scrap of tissue—without ever being noticed.

Cast: Mirai Shida, Ryunosuke Kamiki, Tomokazu Miura, Shinobu Otake
