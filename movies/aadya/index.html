CRAZY RICH ASIANS

Release Date: August 15, 2018 (United States)

Director: Jon M. Chu

Writers: Peter Chiarelli and Adele Lim

Main Cast: Constance Wu, Henry Golding, Michelle Yeoh, Gemma Chan, Awkwafina, Ken Jeong, and Lisa Lu

A world of extravagance, romance, family expectations, and cultural identity. Crazy Rich Asians is more than just a love story; it takes us into a world of unimaginable luxury while exploring the importance of family, tradition, and belonging. Set against the beautiful backdrop of Singapore, the film is filled with stunning fashion, breathtaking locations, and larger-than-life celebrations. What makes it special is the contrast between a glamorous lifestyle and the very real struggles of love, acceptance, and staying true to yourself.
