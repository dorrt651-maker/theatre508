Overview

The Nightmare Before Christmas is a stop-motion animated fantasy film released in 1993. The story follows Jack Skellington, the Pumpkin King of Halloween Town, who discovers Christmas Town and becomes fascinated by the holiday. The movie combines spooky Halloween imagery with the magic of Christmas.
