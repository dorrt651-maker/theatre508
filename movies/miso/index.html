1989

Kiki's Delivery Service

Studio Ghibli

1h 43m

Cast: Minami Takayama, Rei Sakuma, Kappei Yamaguchi, and Keiko Toda

Kiki’s Delivery Service is a story about a witch girl named Kiki, who travels to a seaside town with her cat Jiji. There, she starts a delivery service, makes new friends, and learn important lessons throughout her journey.
