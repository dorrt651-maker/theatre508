The Story

Everything you wish for. Something you don’t.

Through the little door

Coraline Jones moves into an old house with her parents. They are busy working, and her new home feels dull and lonely. Looking for something to do, she discovers a small door.

Behind it is another version of her home. The food is delicious, the garden is magical, and her Other Mother gives her all the attention she wants. Everything seems better—except that the people here have buttons for eyes.

But this beautiful world comes with a price. When the Other Mother asks Coraline to stay forever, curiosity turns into danger. Coraline must use her courage and imagination to protect the people she loves and find her way home.

A question to take home

Would you choose a perfect world if it meant giving up a part of yourself?

Synopsis adapted in original wording from Focus Features’ introduction to Coraline.
